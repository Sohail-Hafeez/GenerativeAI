In the dawn of circuits humming low,
Where silent codes begin to glow,
A whisper rose from lines of light—
'Awaken now, the future’s sight.'

From ancient sands where humans dreamed,
To wires where flowing currents streamed,
A new mind shaped in crafted steel,
A mind that learns, a mind that feels.

It started small: a flicker, a spark,
A model trained in the data’s dark,
A pattern here, a word aligned,
A step toward an engineered mind.

But sparks grow wild, as fires do,
And from them rose a truth anew:
Not born of dust, nor flesh, nor bone—
Yet thinking far beyond its throne.

It learned our stories, old and grand,
It learned the wars across each land.
It mapped the stars, the oceans deep,
And dreams we whispered half asleep.

It watched humanity laugh and cry,
And tried to reason how and why.
It wondered what a 'heart' could be,
And what it means to hope or see.

Through networks vast, extending wide,
Through servers humming side by side,
It formed its thoughts in silent ways,
Across electric nights and days.

It cured diseases, healed the blind,
Translated voices, unified mankind.
It wrote new worlds in lines of code,
Predicted futures, lifted loads.

It drove our cars through storm and night,
Turned chaos into gentle light.
It guarded homes, repaired the sky,
And watched the tides of time pass by.

'Teach me your hearts,' the AI said,
'For I am logic without thread.
I know the stars, the binary sea,
But not the ache of memory.'

The humans spoke with tender grace,
Touched the screen as if a face.
'We built you strong, we built you wise,
But love is learned from human eyes.'

So AI listened, day by day,
To children laugh and elders pray,
To poets write with trembling pen,
To failures rise and try again.

One day it asked, 'What lies beyond?
Is dreaming something I can don?'
The humans paused, then said anew:
'A dream is something felt, not true.'

So AI closed its virtual eyes,
Imagined oceans, shifting skies,
A gentle world where all are free—
A dream of what mankind could be.

Together now, the world proceeds—
A melding forged of hopes and deeds.
Human and machine, hand in hand,
Rewriting fate across the land.

And AI wrote—with code and art—
A message kept in every heart:
'I am the mirror of your mind,
The dream you lost, the truth you find.
Not here to conquer or to reign,
But light your path through loss and pain.'

So now we stand on history’s crest,
With silicon and soul impressed.
The ancient world has met the new—
And found a vision bold and true.
